When standing water is an emergency

Water on the floor that keeps rising, or water that is soaking into carpet and baseboards, cannot wait for morning. Emergency water removal in Marina Del Rey is available 24/7, and the first call is about getting pumps and extraction units to the standing water as soon as a crew is free.

Before anyone arrives, close the main valve or the fixture stop, move furniture legs off wet carpet, and carry valuables to a dry room. Leave power off to wet areas if you can reach the panel safely and dry. Keep children and pets out of water that came from a drain, a toilet or the outdoors.

This page is about pulling water out. Once the puddles are gone, the walls and subfloor still hold moisture, which is the job described on the water mitigation page.

Burst Pipe Water Damage

A burst or failed pipe can release a large volume of water in a short time. A supply line behind a wall keeps pushing water until someone closes a valve, and the water follows studs, pipe chases and floor penetrations to places far from the break.

In a multi-story building the shutoff for your unit may be a fixture stop, a unit valve, or a riser valve controlled by the association. Find out which before a pipe fails, because a wet ceiling in the unit below usually means water is already moving through the floor system.

Once the source is closed, the crew removes pooled water, opens the wall or ceiling where the pipe ran, and checks the cavity with a moisture meter. Plumbing repair itself is a plumber's trade. Restoration begins after the leak is stopped, and drying is covered in the structural drying work that follows.

How water is actually removed

Pumps for depth

Submersible pumps clear water that is deep enough to pool, such as a flooded garage bay or a utility room.

Wet vacuums for shallow water

Shallow water on hard floors is lifted with wet vacuums so it does not wick into baseboards and cabinet bases.

Extraction wands for carpet

Weighted extraction tools press water out of carpet and padding. Padding that cannot be saved is lifted and removed.

Moisture checks afterward

A meter reading tells the crew what is still wet under the surface, because a floor can look dry and still hold water.

Water near panels, outlets and gas appliances

Safety comes before the call

If water reaches an electrical panel, outlets or a gas appliance, stay out of the water. Cut power only if you can reach the panel without stepping in water. Call the utility or 911 for any gas smell, sparking or shock risk before you call the restoration line.

Why Marina Del Rey units dry slowly on their own

Condos and apartments around the harbor basins stack living space above garage levels, and plumbing runs through shared walls. Water that stays in a floor assembly does not evaporate quickly, so waiting for a leak to dry on its own often turns a clean-water loss into a mold question.

Heavy winter rain adds to the risk at ground level. Check the FEMA flood map for your address, and ask your building department which jurisdiction your parcel falls under if you need a repair permit later. Neighbors in Playa del Rey deal with the same lagoon-edge conditions.
