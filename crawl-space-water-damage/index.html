Emergency Crawl Space Water Damage in Marina Del Rey, CA

Water under a raised floor soaks joists, subfloor and insulation, and the crawl space is only part of the drying job.

Who actually has a crawl space here

Many condo towers around the basins have no crawl space at all. They sit on concrete, and a leak there is a different problem. The real cases are raised foundations under older bungalows and small buildings, in places such as Venice and Playa del Rey.

If your home has a vent grate near the ground, a small access hatch in a closet floor or a floor that sits a step above the yard, you may have one. If you live in a ground-floor unit and water came in at the doorway, that is surface water and not a crawl space job. Call and describe what you see.

According to an encyclopedia summary, Venice has older bungalows and early-1900s multifamily buildings. Many of that age were built with raised wood floors, so a plumbing leak or drain failure can send water straight into the dirt and framing below.

What you notice upstairs when the crawl space is wet

Soft or bouncy floor

Wet subfloor loses stiffness, and a spongy patch near a bathroom or kitchen points to water below.

Musty smell from vents

Odor rising through floor registers often means damp air is moving up from the crawl space.

Cold, clammy flooring

Floors over wet insulation or soil stay cool to the touch even on a warm day.

Sagging insulation

Look through the hatch with a flashlight. Insulation hanging down in wet clumps has taken on water.

Mold may follow lingering moisture, but the first job is always the water. If you have health questions about damp air, ask a physician. Our work focuses on finding the moisture and drying it, and our mold remediation page explains what comes next if growth is found.

Safety before anyone goes under the house

  • ✓Do not enter a wet crawl space alone. The space is tight, dark and hard to leave in an emergency.
  • ✓Treat any wiring, junction box or light below the floor as live if water is present. Turn off power at a dry panel first.
  • ✓Shut off the water supply to the building if a pipe is the source.
  • ✓Do not breathe in dust or insulation fibers. Wear gloves and a mask if you must look inside the hatch.
  • ✓Leave standing water that may be sewage alone and call right away.

Crawl spaces are confined, with low clearance and uneven ground. Our crews enter with lighting, protective gear and a plan. If a leak came from a supply line, a plumber stops it. Tell your landlord or association before anyone cuts into floors.

Low ground and old wetland under older homes

The encyclopedia summary says the lower Playa del Rey area was once wetland and dunes, and that the Del Rey Lagoon pond is partly connected to Ballona Creek by an underground pipe. For a homeowner there, soil under the house may stay damp longer than expected, and a pipe leak can linger in the ground after the plumber leaves.

The same summary says wind-blown dunes form the Del Rey Hills, called The Bluffs, rising up to 125 feet above Playa del Rey. Homes on slopes sit differently from homes on flat, low ground. Water may drain toward a crawl space from higher ground in a wet season. Ask a drainage contractor what your lot does.

Venice sits at an elevation of about 10 feet and is very low-lying, according to the encyclopedia summary. Low ground can leave a crawl space collecting water after heavy rain without any pipe failing, so ask the crew to look for the entry point. A sump or drainage fix is a contractor job we do not perform. We dry what got wet.

How we dry a wet crawl space and the floor above

  1. Find the water source

    We look for a leaking supply or drain line, a failed fitting or groundwater, and note it for the plumber.

  2. Pump standing water

    Extraction pumps remove pooled water from the low points so the soil and framing can begin to release moisture.

  3. Remove wet insulation

    Soaked insulation holds water against the subfloor and comes out. Torn or stained vapor barrier is noted.

  4. Dry with air and dehumidifiers

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run in the crawl space, with ducting or equipment above to dry the floor system.

  5. Take readings and recheck

    Moisture meters track subfloor and joists until readings reach a dry standard, and a thermal imaging camera confirms the wet areas are gone.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for water damage restoration, and you can ask any company how its crews apply it. Structural drying of a floor system is slow, and readings on return visits decide when the job ends.

What changes the cost of crawl space water damage

  • ✓How much standing water there is and how long it sat.
  • ✓Whether the crawl space is easy to reach or very low.
  • ✓Insulation, vapor barrier and subfloor damage found.
  • ✓The size of the floor area above that also needs drying.
  • ✓Whether a plumber or drainage contractor must fix the cause first.

Insurers often separate a sudden pipe break from groundwater or seepage. Rising groundwater is generally treated as flood and may need separate coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer how each cause is handled.

Mistakes to avoid and ways to prevent a repeat

  • ✓Do not just run a fan from the hatch and walk away. The soil and joists need real drying.
  • ✓Do not seal a wet crawl space with new insulation or plastic before it is dry.
  • ✓Keep gutters and downspouts pointed away from the foundation.
  • ✓Ask a plumber to check supply and drain lines under the floor after any leak.
  • ✓Look in the hatch after each heavy rain season.

Raised-foundation homes in Venice and Playa del Rey can share these patterns, and an early look under the floor costs little compared with replacing a subfloor.
