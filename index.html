Water Damage Restoration

Water damage restoration for a harbor community

Water damage restoration is the work of stopping water, removing it, drying every wet surface to a measured target and then rebuilding what could not be saved. In Marina Del Rey that work usually happens inside condominiums and apartment buildings around the harbor, where one failed supply line can wet a ceiling below, a shared wall and a hallway at once.

Our crews dispatch around the clock to Marina Del Rey, Venice, Playa Vista, Playa del Rey, Culver City and Santa Monica. The first call decides how much of the building gets wet, so we start with the steps that limit spread: find the source, shut it off, extract standing water and begin water mitigation before drywall and insulation hold the moisture.

After the dry-out we handle the rebuild through water damage repair, so one company carries the job from the first extraction pass to the final coat of paint. If a leak has been hiding for weeks, the same crew can assess mold remediation at the same visit.

Emergency Water Damage

When water damage is an emergency in Marina Del Rey, and what to do first

If water is still moving, treat it as an emergency. A burst supply line, an overflowing toilet, a failed water heater or a washing machine hose that let go can put hundreds of gallons into a unit in the time it takes to find the valve. Call the 24/7 line first, then do what you safely can while we mobilize.

  • ✓Shut off the water at the fixture valve or the unit main. Ask building management where the riser shutoff is if you cannot find it.
  • ✓Turn off power to wet areas only if you can reach the panel without standing in water. If the panel is wet or you smell gas, leave and call 911 and the utility first.
  • ✓Lift valuables, electronics and papers above the wet line and move rugs off damp floors.
  • ✓Keep people and pets out of any water that may be contaminated, including sewage and storm drain backup.
  • ✓Take photos and short videos of the damage before anything is moved. Your insurer and association will ask for them.

Standing water that is deeper than a shallow film needs pumps, which is the work of emergency water removal. Water that came from outside, from a storm or a backed-up drain, falls under flood damage restoration and often carries contamination that changes how crews protect themselves and dispose of materials.

Decision Guide

Which kind of water is it, and what happens next

Restoration crews classify water by source, because the source decides how risky it is to touch and which materials can be saved. The categories below come from the industry standard, ANSI/IICRC S500, which any company can be asked about.

Water categoryTypical sourceWhat usually happens
Category 1Supply line, faucet or tub overflow with clean waterExtraction, drying and, if caught early, most materials are kept
Category 2Dishwasher or washing machine discharge, toilet overflow without solidsExtraction, cleaning and antimicrobial treatment, porous materials often removed
Category 3Sewage, storm drain backup, rising floodwaterContainment, removal of affected porous materials, disinfection and protective gear

Clean water becomes worse water with time. A Category 1 leak left to sit can be treated as Category 2 after a day or two, which is one reason to call early rather than wait to see whether the carpet dries on its own.

Cost And Insurance

What shapes the cost of a water damage job

We do not quote a number over the phone because the same leak can be a small job or a large one. Five things move the scope: the water category, how many square feet are wet, which materials absorbed it, how many days of drying equipment the structure needs and how much has to be rebuilt afterward.

Once a crew has measured the damage, you get the scope and the price in plain terms before work starts. Want your real number? Call the 24/7 line and describe the loss.

Insurance in general terms

Sudden and accidental water damage is often covered by a homeowner or renter policy, while gradual leaks and neglected maintenance often are not. Flood damage is usually excluded and needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy and ask your insurer what applies, and in a condominium ask the association which parts of the building it insures. We document the loss with photos, moisture readings and a written scope so that conversation has facts behind it.

Local Knowledge

What is different about water damage around the harbor

Marina Del Rey is an unincorporated community of Los Angeles County, and the County of Los Angeles owns the marina, which its Department of Beaches and Harbors manages. That matters when a repair needs permits, because building services for unincorporated communities come from County Public Works Building and Safety, while a neighboring address inside the City of Los Angeles answers to LADBS. Confirm which one covers your parcel before a rebuild starts.

Many buildings around the eight basins are multi-story and share walls, floors and risers. A leak in an upper unit rarely stays there, so we map moisture in the units below and beside the source, not only the unit that called. The Admiralty High-Rise, with about 800 condominiums just across the border in the City of Los Angeles, is an example of how far one riser can reach.

Storm water has its own route. Ballona Creek is a roughly nine-mile flood protection channel draining a watershed of about 130 square miles, and the 2024 atmospheric river storms made Los Angeles one of its wettest months on record. Ground-floor units and garages near the basins and the creek should check the FEMA flood map for their address and ask their insurer whether flood coverage is in place.

Our dispatch covers the main approaches, Lincoln Boulevard, Washington Boulevard and Admiralty Way, and the harbor streets in between, from Fisherman's Village to the neighborhoods along Ballona Lagoon. Tell us your building and floor when you call so we can plan access and parking with management.
