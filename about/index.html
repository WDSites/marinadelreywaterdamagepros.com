About Marina Del Rey Water Damage Pros

A 24/7 water damage restoration and mold remediation service for Marina Del Rey and the nearby towns of Venice, Playa Vista, Playa del Rey, Culver City and Santa Monica.

What we do

Marina Del Rey Water Damage Pros answers the phone when water is where it should not be. We dispatch licensed and insured restoration professionals to extract water, dry structures, clean and sanitize, rebuild damaged materials and assess mold that follows a leak. The office address is 4025 Lincoln Blvd in Marina del Rey, and the dispatch line is answered by a person at every hour, because a recorded message does not stop a leak.

Our focus is residential and multi-unit buildings around the harbor and the Westside, where condominiums, apartments, older bungalows and newer townhomes each fail in different ways. A supply line failure in a condominium and a storm drain backup in a garage are different jobs, and we treat them that way.

The standards we work to

Water damage work in the United States follows two published standards from the Institute of Inspection, Cleaning and Restoration Certification. ANSI/IICRC S500 covers professional water damage restoration, including water categories and drying goals. ANSI/IICRC S520 covers professional mold remediation. We describe our process in those terms, and we encourage you to ask any company, including us, whether its crews work to them.

How a job runs

The first call is a conversation, not a form. You describe the damage and we tell you what to shut off, what to photograph and what to leave alone. A crew assesses with moisture meters and thermal imaging, explains the scope and how the price is set before work starts, and then begins extraction and drying. Moisture readings are logged on each visit so the dry-out is measured, not assumed.

Afterward you have photos, readings and a written scope. Those help your insurer, your landlord or your homeowners association understand what happened and what was done, though we never promise what an insurer will approve.

What to do while you wait for a crew

  • ✓Shut off the water at the fixture or main valve if you can reach it safely.
  • ✓Leave power on in dry areas, and turn it off to wet areas only if the panel is dry and safe to reach.
  • ✓Lift valuables off wet floors and keep people and pets out of contaminated water.

The buildings we see most

Around the harbor, most calls come from multi-story condominium and apartment buildings with shared walls, floors and risers. A leak in one unit travels, so a call about a stained ceiling is often a call about the unit above, and sometimes about the one above that. We ask who owns the plumbing, who manages access and which units sit below, because those answers decide how fast a crew can reach the source.

Toward Venice and Santa Monica the mix shifts to older bungalows and small multifamily buildings, and toward Playa Vista it shifts to newer townhomes and mid-rise buildings. The service area page explains how each town differs, and water damage repair covers what happens when the rebuild begins.

What to expect on the day

A crew arrives with moisture meters, a thermal imaging camera, extraction units, air movers and LGR dehumidifiers. The first hour is spent finding where the water went, not only where it shows. Wet materials that cannot be dried come out, the drying equipment goes in and you receive a plain explanation of what each machine is doing and when it will be checked.

We tell you when the drying is finished by showing the readings, not by guessing. If the damage turns out larger than the first look suggested, we explain the change before continuing, and you decide how to proceed.

What we do not do

  • ✓We do not handle fire and smoke damage.
  • ✓We do not offer basement waterproofing or foundation repair. If water keeps coming in, that is a different trade, and we will say so.
  • ✓We do not rent drying equipment. Air movers and dehumidifiers run as part of a job.
  • ✓We do not give health advice about mold. Health questions belong to a physician.
