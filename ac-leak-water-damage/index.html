Why an air conditioner leaks water into the building

An air conditioner pulls moisture out of indoor air and sends it down a small drain line. When that line clogs with slime or debris, the pan under the coil fills and spills. The water lands on whatever sits below, which is often a ceiling, a closet floor or the inside of a wall.

Here is a plain admission. How much air conditioning runs this close to the coast depends on the building and the season, and we will not guess at numbers. The real local cases are condensate and ductless leaks in condos and apartments, plus the occasional air handler tucked into a hall closet or ceiling space.

Repairing the refrigerant side, the coil or the blower is an HVAC contractor job. We handle what the water touched: finding it, drying it and restoring the surfaces. Once the structure is dry, water damage repair covers the rebuild.

A stain tells you where water came out, not where it started. Water follows joists and pipe holes first. A moisture meter and a thermal imaging camera help us trace it back to the source.

First-hour safety when an air conditioner is leaking

  • ✓Turn the thermostat to off and cut power to the unit at its switch or breaker. Water and the blower motor do not mix.
  • ✓Stay out from under a bulging ceiling. A sagging panel can hold a lot of water and can drop without warning.
  • ✓Do not touch outlets, fixtures or fans near the wet area. If the panel is in a wet space, call an electrician.
  • ✓Set a bucket under any steady drip and move furniture and electronics aside.
  • ✓Photograph the stain, the unit and the pan before anything changes.

Condensate is not supply water, so you usually cannot stop it at a valve. If you are unsure whether the source is a drain line or a pipe, shut off the unit first and then watch whether the drip continues. A drip that keeps coming with the unit off points to a supply line instead.

The county Public Works waterworks page says most water in the county-owned Marina del Rey Water System arrives through County Waterworks District 29, which buys nearly all its supply from the Metropolitan Water District through West Basin Municipal Water District. It can also draw from LADWP through two emergency interconnections. Your water bill names your utility, so call that one for any main-side shutoff.

How we dry out after an AC leak

  1. Trace the water

    We read walls, ceilings and floors with moisture meters and a thermal imaging camera to find every wet area, including ones that look normal.

  2. Protect and open

    Contents are lifted or covered. Wet insulation and soft drywall come out, and small access cuts let air reach the cavity.

  3. Set drying equipment

    Air movers and LGR dehumidifiers run in a closed space so moisture leaves the structure instead of drifting to the next room.

  4. Check the readings

    We return to take new readings and adjust the equipment until the materials reach a dry standard.

  5. Hand off the repair

    With the cavity dry, patching and paint follow, and your HVAC contractor services the unit.

ANSI/IICRC S500 is the published standard for professional water damage restoration, and you can ask any company how its crews apply it. Wet drywall is often better cut out cleanly than left to sag, as our drywall water damage repair page explains.

Stacked units and shared ceilings around the basins

The encyclopedia summary of county data says the harbor has eight basins separated by six strips of land. Many of the condo and apartment buildings sit around those basins, with units stacked on top of each other. A drain line that overflows in an upper unit can wet the ceiling of the unit below before anyone upstairs notices.

In practice, tell your neighbor and your association the same day, and ask who owns the air handler, drain line and ceiling cavity. The same applies to larger towers such as the Admiralty High-Rise, which the same encyclopedia summary describes as having about 800 condominiums and sitting just across the line in the City of Los Angeles. Confirm which jurisdiction your parcel is in.

Condensate problems also show up in newer buildings in Playa Vista and in renter-heavy Santa Monica apartments, where units share ceiling space.

What changes the cost of an AC leak dry-out

We cannot name a number before seeing the damage. These factors move the scope:

  • ✓How long the drain line leaked before anyone noticed.
  • ✓Whether the water stayed in the ceiling or ran into walls and a floor below.
  • ✓Insulation type, since wet batts usually come out and do not dry well.
  • ✓Texture, paint and finish work needed after the structure is dry.

Insurance questions for a slow condensate leak

Insurers often treat sudden and accidental water differently from water that seeped for a long time. A pan that cracked overnight and a drain line that dripped for months can be read very differently. Check your policy, take dated photographs and ask your insurer how it handles each case. Condo owners should also ask the association which parts its policy covers.

Keeping the drain line clear

  • ✓Ask an HVAC technician to flush the condensate line at each seasonal service.
  • ✓Look at the overflow pan now and then for standing water or rust.
  • ✓Check ductless units for drips after the first hot days of the season.
  • ✓Ask your association whether a float switch is installed that cuts power when the pan fills.
