Marina Del Rey and the towns around it

Marina Del Rey is an unincorporated community of Los Angeles County, ZIP 90292, surrounded by the City of Los Angeles. Water damage does not respect those lines, but permits, utilities and building departments do, so each nearby town has its own page with the details that matter for a repair there.

The five nearby towns we serve

Venice, ZIPs 90291 and 90292, sits just northwest of the harbor. Its older bungalows, walk streets and canal-area homes are low-lying, and water damage there often involves aging pipes and wood floors. The page covers LADWP, LADBS and the ZIPs we cover.

Playa Vista, ZIP 90094, is a newer community built on the former Hughes Aircraft site beginning in the early 2000s, so supply lines, appliances and shared systems matter more than old plumbing. Playa del Rey, ZIP 90293, lies south of the harbor between the beach, the lagoons and the bluffs, with a coastal setting that shapes its moisture problems.

Culver City, ZIPs 90230 and 90232, is its own incorporated city with its own Building Safety Division, and Ballona Creek runs through it. Santa Monica, ZIPs 90401 through 90405, is also its own city with its own water division and permit center, and most of its homes are rented, which changes who has to be told about a leak.

Nearby places we mention but do not build pages for

Calls also come in from the Marina Peninsula, which sits within Venice, from Westchester and from El Segundo near the south end of the coast, and from the Del Rey and Palms neighborhoods of Los Angeles near Culver City. If your address is in one of them, call the line and we will confirm coverage for your block.

Why the town matters to the repair

Water extraction and drying look the same in every town, but the paperwork around the rebuild does not. A drywall and flooring repair in Culver City may involve the Building Safety Division, while the same repair in Venice falls to LADBS, and a Marina Del Rey address in the unincorporated county answers to County Public Works. Confirm which body covers your parcel before ordering materials.

Utilities differ too. The county runs a small Marina del Rey water system for the harbor leaseholds, the City of Santa Monica runs its own water division, and many Culver City addresses are served by Golden State Water Company. Your water bill shows who serves your address, and that is who to call about a main line or a meter.

If you manage more than one building

Property managers and associations often call about several addresses at once, for example after a storm. Give the dispatcher each building, the affected floors and any access rules. We document each address separately so each claim and each association file stays clean, and we explain the scope and price per building before work starts.
