Newer buildings, different leaks

Redevelopment of Playa Vista began in 2001 or 2002, so most buildings are roughly a quarter century old or newer. That changes the failure list. Corroded old pipe is less common than a washing machine hose, an ice maker line, a water heater fitting or a balcony that stopped shedding water.

Newer homes are also built tight. Sealed windows, insulated walls and finished garages hold moisture in, so a leak that would dry out in an older drafty house can stay wet inside a Playa Vista wall unless someone dries it on purpose.

Where condo and townhome leaks travel

Shared stacks, common walls and floor-to-ceiling plumbing chases let water move from one unit to the next. A supply line failure on an upper floor can show up as a stain two floors down. Walk the lower units if you can, because the wet area is often bigger than the visible one.

Condo owners should ask the association which parts of the building it insures and which parts belong to the owner policy. That answer decides who calls whom first, and it is much easier to get before a claim than after.

When water is spreading in Playa Vista

Call our 24/7 line the moment water is moving across a floor or dripping from a ceiling. Playa Vista parks and landscaped areas are irrigated with recycled water, so tell us if the source is an irrigation line, and keep pets and children out of that water until the crew has looked at it.

We offer same-day response when available and a crew equipped with extraction units, moisture meters, air movers and low-grain refrigerant dehumidifiers. We never promise a specific arrival time.

Before we arrive in Playa Vista

Shut it off

Find the unit shutoff under the sink or at the water heater, or ask building management to close the riser.

Breaker check

Turn off the circuit for wet rooms only when the panel is dry and clear of water. If not, call the utility or 911.

Move valuables

Lift electronics, rugs and paperwork up and out of the wet zone, and photograph each item first.

Stay out of dirty water

Do not wade through water from drains or outdoor lines. Keep children and pets away and wait for the crew.

Boundaries, parks and the places that matter for water

Ballona Creek forms the northern boundary of Playa Vista, Lincoln Boulevard runs along the west edge and Centinela Avenue along the east. Ballona Creek is a roughly nine-mile flood protection channel, so if your home or office is close to it, check the FEMA flood map for your address and ask about flood coverage.

Bluff Creek Drive, the Bluff Creek riparian corridor and Ballona Discovery Park sit along the southeast and the Ballona Wetlands bound the west. Garden-level units and ground-floor offices near these green areas are the ones that benefit most from a quick moisture check after heavy rain.

Runway Playa Vista and the offices and studios around it are part of Silicon Beach. A burst sprinkler or restroom line in a mixed-use building can wet the shop below and the unit above, so commercial managers should call before they move stock.

How we sequence the work here

In sealed newer buildings, drying is the priority, so water mitigation leads and a moisture map shows where cavities stay wet. Standing water goes first through emergency water removal, and soaked drywall or insulation leaves through water damage cleanup.

Once the readings come down, water damage repair brings back the drywall, cabinetry and flooring. If water came from outdoors, flood damage restoration applies. Neighbors to the south can read about Playa del Rey, and our east side coverage includes Culver City.

Cost factors and insurance, in general terms

Scope depends on the water category, the area that got wet, the materials involved, how many drying days the structure needs and how much rebuild follows. We explain scope and price before work starts, and we cannot give a number without seeing the damage.

Sudden and accidental damage is often covered and slow leaks often are not. Flood damage usually needs separate flood coverage. Check your policy, ask your insurer, and never rely on us or anyone else to promise an outcome.

Utilities and permits in Playa Vista

Playa Vista is in the City of Los Angeles, so water service is LADWP and permits go through LADBS. Ask LADBS whether your repair needs a permit and confirm your parcel's jurisdiction before work starts.
